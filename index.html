Venetian

Slice a picture into strips that reassemble into one image on an X multi-photo post.

What this is

X supports up to 20 images per post. Upload one wide picture, cut into vertical strips, in order, and the multi-photo grid stitches them back into a single poster, a scroll-across effect people will ask you about. Venetian does the cutting: the geometry that makes the seams invisible, and nothing else.

  1. Drop a wide picture (roughly 8:5 or wider works best).
  2. Pick a strip count. 10 is the sweet spot; 20 is the venetian-blind look.
  3. Download the ZIP and drag the numbered files into the X composer in order.

Your image never leaves this page. All slicing happens locally in your browser.

PNG · JPG · WEBP. Anything your browser can decode.

How it will read in the grid

Files are numbered; upload order is the effect. X can't reorder after posting.
